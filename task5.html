Midterm Project Proposal: Gaming Hub

Project Description

Gaming Hub is a centralized web platform designed for video game enthusiasts. The purpose of this website is to provide a unified space where gamers can discover trending games, read industry news, and share reviews. The target audience includes teenagers and adult gamers (ages 12–35) seeking reliable game recommendations and community interaction.

Sitemap (Navigation Structure)

  1. Home Page (Featured games, hero banner, recent news highlights)
  2. Games Catalog (Grid layout of games, filter by genre/platform)
  3. Game Reviews & Ratings (Community scores, detailed text reviews)
  4. News & Blog (Latest updates in the gaming industry, eSports news)
  5. Contact & Community Feedback (Support form, join newsletter)
