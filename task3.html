Hideo Kojima

A visionary video game designer and auteur

About Hideo Kojima

Born on August 24, 1963, Hideo Kojima is a Japanese video game designer, director, and producer. He is widely considered an auteur of video games, pioneering the stealth action genre and bringing cinematic storytelling into interactive entertainment.

Important Life Events:

  1. 1986: Joined Konami as a designer and planner.
  2. 1987: Released the first Metal Gear game, inventing the stealth genre.
  3. 2005: Founded Kojima Productions (originally within Konami).
  4. 2015: Left Konami and established an independent Kojima Productions.

Key Contributions & Inventions:

  • Created the globally acclaimed Metal Gear Solid franchise.
  • Introduced deep, movie-like cutscenes into gaming mechanics.
  • Developed the "Strand Game" genre with the release of Death Stranding.
